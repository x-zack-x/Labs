# The Crime Lab

## Category
Endpoint Forensics

## Scenario
We're currently in the midst of a murder investigation, and we've obtained the victim's phone as a key piece of evidence. After conducting interviews with witnesses and those in the victim's inner circle, your objective is to meticulously analyze the information we've gathered and diligently trace the evidence to piece together the sequence of events leading up to the incident.

## Tools Used
- **ALEAPP** 


## Walkthrough

### Q1 — Based on the accounts of the witnesses and individuals close to the victim, it has become clear that the victim was interested in trading. This has led him to invest all of his money and acquire debt. Can you identify the SHA256 of the trading application the victim primarily used on his phone?

![reporthome](screenshots/1.jpeg)

**What I did:** After accessing the reports generated by ALEAPP, I started navigating through the report sections to understand its structure. From there, I located the applications section.

**What I found:** The "Installed APPS" section contained three sub-sections, one of which had a table of applications installed via Google Play Store (GMS).

**Answer:** `4f168a772350f283a1c49e78c1548d7c2c6c05106d8b9feb825fdc3466e9df3c`

![hashvalue](screenshots/2.jpeg)


### Q2 — According to the testimony of the victim's best friend, he said, "While we were together, my friend got several calls he avoided. He said he owed the caller a lot of money but couldn't repay now". How much does the victim owe this person?

**What I did:** The report had two sub-sections for messages from communication apps. I started by searching in the "Discord Chats" sub-section, then I checked the second sub-section, "SMS messages".

**What I found:** The latter contained a single message mentioning the loan.

**Answer:** `250 000 (EGP)`

![ransom](screenshots/3.jpeg)


### Q3 — What is the name of the person to whom the victim owes money?

![numéro](screenshots/4.jpeg)

**What I did:** It is important to note that witness statements highlighted that the lender called multiple times. Therefore, I checked the "Call Logs" section to find the lender's number. I noticed a number repeating several times without any answer: `+20 11 72 13 72 58`.

**What I found:** I used the identified number to filter the victim's contacts.

**Answer:** `Shady Wahab`

![name](screenshots/5.jpeg)



### Q4 — Based on the statement from the victim's family, they said that on September 20, 2023, he departed from his residence without informing anyone of his destination. Where was the victim located at that moment?

**What I did:** To trace the victim's activity, I used the "Recent Activity" section.

**What I found:** I found a table containing the requested date with a Google Maps screenshot.

**Answer:** `The Nile Ritz-Carlton`

![map](screenshots/6.jpeg)



### Q5 — The detective continued his investigation by questioning the hotel lobby. She informed him that the victim had reserved the room for 10 days and had a flight scheduled thereafter. The investigator believes that the victim may have stored his ticket information on his phone. Look for where the victim intended to travel.

**What I did:** I started by inspecting the "Recent Activity" sub-section, but it didn't contain activity for the specific date. However, I found a digital photo of a flight ticket departing from Cairo, Egypt, which confirmed the victim's location.

**What I found:** This ticket contained the destination I was looking for, with a date that fully matches the hotel lobby's testimony (01.10.2023).

**Answer:** `Las Vegas`

![map](screenshots/7.jpeg)



### Q6 — After examining the victim's Discord conversations, we discovered he had arranged to meet a friend at a specific location. Can you determine where this meeting was supposed to occur?

**What I did:** I accessed the "Discord Chats" sub-section to look for the relevant message.

**What I found:** I found a message from "rob1ns0n" on "20.09.2023" containing the meeting location.

**Answer:** `The Mob Museum`

![museum](screenshots/8.jpeg)

## What I Learned
- I learned that ALEAPP is a powerful forensic tool capable of parsing and extracting critical evidence from Android storage while automatically classifying data (SMS, Calls, App activity).
- Using a private browser and disk encryption is essential for personal data protection to prevent unauthorized data collection.
